Our Process

We follow a systematic 10-step approach to ensure every project is delivered on time, on budget, and to specification.

1. Initial Consultation

Discuss your project goals, budget, timeline, and site conditions.

2. Site Assessment

Conduct detailed site inspection and measurements.

3. Design & Material Selection

Recommend materials and design solutions tailored to your requirements.

4. Quote Preparation

Provide detailed cost estimate including materials and labour.

5. Project Planning

Schedule timeline, arrange access, and coordinate logistics.

6. Material Procurement

Source and arrange delivery of all required materials.

7. Fabrication Prep

Prepare and organize materials for efficient installation.

8. Installation Execution

Professional on-site installation by experienced teams.

9. Quality Inspection

Comprehensive quality check and site cleanup.

10. Handover & Warranty

Final documentation, manuals, and warranty provision.
